ADHD • NEW YORK

Adult ADHD Care

ADHD can affect attention, organization, time management, impulse control, and follow-through. An individualized assessment helps clarify your concerns and options.

Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is a neurodevelopmental condition. Adults may experience distractibility, forgetfulness, difficulty organizing tasks, restlessness, impulsivity, or challenges managing time. Similar symptoms can also occur with sleep problems, anxiety, depression, substance use, or medical conditions, so assessment matters.

ASSESSMENT & CARE

Assessment and planning

An adult ADHD assessment may review current concerns, childhood and school history where available, functioning across settings, sleep, mood, medical history, and other possible explanations. Care options depend on the findings and may include skills-based strategies, psychotherapy, and medication evaluation or management when appropriate.

NEXT STEPS

Practical support

Treatment goals may include building routines, improving task planning, and addressing related emotional or sleep concerns. Medication decisions require an individualized discussion of benefits, risks, alternatives, and monitoring.
