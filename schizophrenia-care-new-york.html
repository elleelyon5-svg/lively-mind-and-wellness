SCHIZOPHRENIA • NEW YORK

Schizophrenia Assessment and Care

Schizophrenia can affect perception, thinking, emotions, and functioning. Coordinated care and individualized assessment are important.

Schizophrenia is a mental health condition that may involve psychosis, such as hallucinations or delusions, as well as changes in thinking, motivation, emotional expression, or functioning. Symptoms and support needs vary. A thorough assessment is important because medical conditions, substances, and other psychiatric conditions can cause similar experiences.

ASSESSMENT & CARE

Assessment and coordinated support

A psychiatric evaluation may review symptoms over time, medical and medication history, sleep, substance use, safety, and day-to-day functioning. Treatment often requires coordinated, ongoing care and may include antipsychotic medication management, psychosocial support, and collaboration with other members of a care team. The appropriate level of care depends on clinical needs.

NEXT STEPS

When urgent care is needed

New or worsening psychosis, inability to meet basic needs, severe confusion, or safety concerns warrant prompt professional assessment. If there is immediate danger, call 911 or go to an emergency department. In the United States, call or text 988 for crisis support. This practice does not provide emergency services.
