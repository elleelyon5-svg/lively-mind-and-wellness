PSYCHOTHERAPY • NEW YORK

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

CBT is a structured, collaborative therapy that explores how thoughts, feelings, and behaviors can influence one another.

Cognitive behavioral therapy often focuses on recognizing unhelpful thinking and behavior patterns and practicing alternative responses. Goals and techniques are tailored to the person, concerns, and preferences. CBT may be used for a range of concerns, but suitability depends on an individual assessment.

ASSESSMENT & CARE

What sessions may involve

Depending on your goals, therapy may include identifying patterns, setting practical goals, learning coping skills, trying between-session exercises, and reviewing what is helpful. Progress is collaborative, and the pace and methods can be adjusted to your needs.

NEXT STEPS

Personalized therapy options

CBT is not a one-size-fits-all approach and outcomes vary. Your provider can discuss whether CBT or another form of therapy may be appropriate alongside or separate from psychiatric care.
