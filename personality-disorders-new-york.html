PERSONALITY DISORDERS • NEW YORK

Personality Disorder Assessment and Care

Long-standing patterns in emotions, relationships, self-image, or behavior can cause distress. A respectful assessment can help clarify needs and treatment options.

Personality disorders describe enduring patterns of inner experience and behavior that may affect relationships, emotional regulation, self-image, and daily functioning. Experiences vary widely, and a diagnosis requires a comprehensive clinical assessment rather than a single symptom or online checklist.

ASSESSMENT & CARE

Individualized, respectful assessment

A clinician may explore relationship patterns, mood and coping, personal history, current stressors, functioning, and other possible explanations for symptoms. Care planning depends on individual needs and may include psychotherapy, skills development, medication management for specific symptoms when appropriate, and coordination with other providers.

NEXT STEPS

Change can be supported

Treatment goals may include strengthening coping skills, improving communication, and managing distress. The best approach depends on the person and the specific concerns; no single treatment fits everyone. If you are in immediate danger or crisis, call 911 or call/text 988 in the United States.
