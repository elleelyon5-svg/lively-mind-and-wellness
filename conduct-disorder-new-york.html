CHILD & ADOLESCENT MENTAL HEALTH

Conduct Disorder: Information for Families

Persistent patterns of aggression, serious rule violations, or behavior that harms others may warrant a careful child and adolescent mental health assessment.

Conduct disorder is a condition diagnosed in children or adolescents when a persistent pattern of behavior violates others' rights or major age-appropriate rules. Assessment should consider the young person's developmental stage, context, safety, family and school experiences, and other possible mental health or learning needs. Only a qualified clinician can make a diagnosis.

ASSESSMENT & CARE

Seeking an appropriate assessment

Families may begin with the child's pediatrician, a licensed child and adolescent mental health clinician, or a school support team. Evaluation can help identify contributing factors and appropriate family, behavioral, educational, or clinical supports. Early, coordinated support can be important.
