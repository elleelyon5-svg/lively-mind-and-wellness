EATING DISORDERS • NEW YORK

Eating Disorder Support

Eating disorders can affect physical health, emotions, and relationships. Compassionate, timely assessment can help people connect with appropriate care.

Eating disorders may involve restrictive eating, binge eating, purging, intense concerns about weight or shape, or distressing patterns around food. They can affect people at any body size and may carry serious medical risks. This page is educational and does not replace an eating-disorder-specific assessment.

ASSESSMENT & CARE

Finding the right level of care

Care often benefits from a coordinated team that may include clinicians with eating-disorder expertise, medical providers, and registered dietitians. The appropriate plan depends on symptoms, medical stability, and individual needs. Lively Mind and Wellness can discuss concerns and whether services are appropriate, but specialized or higher-level care may be needed.

NEXT STEPS

Medical safety matters

Fainting, chest pain, confusion, severe weakness, dehydration, or other concerning physical symptoms require urgent medical evaluation. Call 911 for an emergency or go to the nearest emergency department. If you are unsure where to start, contact a qualified healthcare professional promptly.
