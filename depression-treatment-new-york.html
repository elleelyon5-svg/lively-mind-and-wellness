DEPRESSION • NEW YORK

Depression Care

Depression can affect mood, energy, sleep, concentration, relationships, and everyday functioning. Support starts with understanding your experience.

Depression can look different from person to person. Some people notice persistent sadness or loss of interest; others experience irritability, fatigue, changes in sleep or appetite, difficulty concentrating, or feelings of hopelessness. A clinical assessment helps explore symptoms, their duration, their impact, and possible contributing factors.

ASSESSMENT & CARE

Assessment shaped around you

An evaluation may review your mood, sleep, energy, medical history, medications, stressors, and goals. Care options depend on the assessment and may include psychotherapy, medication evaluation or management, practical coping strategies, or coordination with other clinicians.

NEXT STEPS

When to seek help

Consider reaching out when symptoms persist, interfere with daily life, or feel difficult to manage alone. If you may harm yourself or someone else, call or text 988 in the United States, call 911 for immediate danger, or go to the nearest emergency department. This practice does not provide emergency services.
