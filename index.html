FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept insurance?

Lively Mind and Wellness is a private-pay practice. A superbill may be provided for patients who wish to seek out-of-network reimbursement.

Is care provided by telehealth?

Yes. Visits are conducted virtually when appropriate and permitted by applicable law.

What if I am experiencing an emergency?

This practice does not provide emergency services. Call 911, go to the nearest emergency department, or call/text 988 in the United States for crisis support.
